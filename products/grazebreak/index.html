1. Overview

The iOS application "GRAZE BREAK" (the "App") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting personal information. The App does not require account registration or login, and the developer does not collect or store directly identifiable personal data (such as your name, email address, or street address) on any proprietary server.

Your game progress (scores, coins, unlocked stages, pilots, upgrades, and settings) is stored locally on your device only.

2. Third-Party Services & Data Collected

The App uses the following third-party services (SDKs) to deliver advertisements, analyze gameplay usage, provide leaderboards, and process in-app purchases. These services may automatically collect and process device identifiers, advertising data, approximate location, in-app interactions, and crash/diagnostic data.

  • Google AdMob (Google LLC): Used to serve and measure banner, interstitial, and rewarded advertisements.
    Google Privacy Policy / How AdMob uses data
  • Unity Ads (Unity Technologies): An advertising partner that delivers ads through Google AdMob mediation (banner, interstitial, and rewarded).
    Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy
  • Firebase Analytics (Google LLC): Used for anonymous usage statistics to track stage progression, improve game balance, and diagnose issues.
    Privacy and Security in Firebase
  • Apple Game Center (Apple Inc.): Used when you choose to sign in, to display leaderboards (BLITZ / FULL RUN / DAILY / PROTOCOL) and submit scores.
    Apple Privacy Policy
  • Apple StoreKit (In-App Purchase): Used to process and restore the one-time "Ad-Free Pack" purchase. Payment information is handled exclusively by Apple and is never accessible to the developer.

3. Tracking (ATT) & Ad Consent

To deliver relevant advertisements, the App displays Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) prompt once after your first launch.

You can play the full game even if you choose "Ask App Not to Track"—non-personalized ads will be shown instead. You can change your tracking preference anytime in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

Users in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland, and U.S. states with applicable privacy laws are shown a consent message provided by Google (Google User Messaging Platform) before ads are loaded. You can review or change your choices anytime from Settings > Privacy > "Ad privacy settings" in the App.

If you purchase the one-time "Ad-Free Pack", banner and interstitial ads are removed and the tracking prompt and consent message are no longer shown.

4. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed toward children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without parental consent.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time due to legal changes or app updates. Any significant changes will be posted on this page.

3. トラッキング（ATT）と広告の同意について

本アプリは、広告配信の最適化のため、初回起動後に iOS の App Tracking Transparency（ATT）によるトラッキング許可ダイアログを表示します。

許可しない場合でも本アプリは問題なくプレイでき、パーソナライズされていない広告が表示されます。トラッキング設定は、いつでも iOS の「設定」＞「プライバシーとセキュリティ」＞「トラッキング」から変更できます。

欧州経済領域（EEA）、英国、スイス、およびプライバシー関連法が適用される米国の州のユーザーには、広告を読み込む前に Google が提供する同意メッセージ（Google User Messaging Platform）を表示します。選択内容は、アプリ内の「設定」＞「プライバシー」＞「広告のプライバシー設定」からいつでも確認・変更できます。

買い切りの「広告削除パック」をご購入いただいた場合、バナーおよびインタースティシャル広告は非表示となり、トラッキング許可ダイアログと同意メッセージも表示されなくなります。
